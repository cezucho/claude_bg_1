Candidate boards — same scale

Team A (blue) plays up from the bottom, team B (red) is its mirror image. Symbols are the five roles on their start hexes; dashed hexes are spawns; tinted hexes the nexus; tower shapes the five towers. Numbers: 400 AI-vs-AI matches each with towers solid, walkable in brackets.
Contact: hexes from each champion to its nearest enemy when the opening ends. Patterns live: ready rotatable/fixed abilities that have something to hit on the ladder. Direct kills: matches ending with a direct attack on the nexus rather than siege at round close. Comebacks: the winner trailed by 5+ nexus HP at some round close. All champions are today's placeholders, whose openings were written for the classic board — on the new boards most openings hit the fallback, so opening behaviour is not compared.
